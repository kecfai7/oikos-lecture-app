## Slide 05: THE INTERFACE TRAP: OVERCOMING THE CASINO OF RANDOMNESS
**Subtitle:** Endless 'slot-machine' prompt regeneration vs. engineering structured constraint pipelines

### 🎙️ English Lecture Script

Look at Slide 5 for a vital warning: "The Interface Trap: Overcoming the Casino of Randomness."

The most dangerous pitfall in AI video is treating the interface like a casino slot machine! Look at the left: Creators type a prompt, dislike the result, and hit "Regenerate" twenty-five times hoping for a lucky roll! This burns hundreds of dollars in API credits!

Look at the right: The Intelligence Architect Mindset! 

We establish Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). We freeze character faces with image anchors and program exact camera coordinate vectors *before* rendering! This guarantees predictable, studio-grade results in just 3 passes!

### 🇰🇷 Korean Teaching Guide (강의 가이드)
- **강의 요약:** 인터페이스의 덫: 슬롯머신 도박식 생성 대 엔지니어링 통제 파이프라인
- **핵심 포인트:**
  - Left (카지노의 덫): 프롬프트 넣고 마음에 안 들면 '다시 생성'을 25번씩 누르며 크레딧과 시간을 날리는 도박식 낭비
  - Right (엔지니어링 통제): 렌더링 전 캐릭터 얼굴과 조명 참조 앵커를 먼저 고정하고 수학적 카메라 경로 주입
